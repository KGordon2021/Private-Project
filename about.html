Our story

Built by people who've stood on the dock at 4am.

Meridian was founded by two freight clerks who were tired of watching containers miss their sailing over a missing signature. We still run the business the same way: documentation gets done a day early, not an hour before the gate closes.

Today we're a licensed customs broker and IATA cargo agent with our own bonded warehouse, but the standard is the same one we started with — no shipment leaves without a name attached to it.

2009

Founded at the Port of Rotterdam

Started as a two-person customs filing desk serving small textile exporters.

2013

First bonded warehouse opens

4,200m² facility added for consolidation, palletising and export packing.

2017

Air freight desk launches

IATA accreditation secured; daily consolidated flights added for time-critical cargo.

Agent network now spans five continents, unified under a single tracking platform.

What we hold to

The four lines we won't cross.

Documentation first

Paperwork is filed before the cargo is loaded — never after. It's the single biggest cause of delay we've eliminated.

One point of contact

Rates you can trust

No surcharges added after booking. What we quote against your Incoterms is what you're invoiced.

Compliance, not shortcuts

Correct HS classification every time, even when a faster filing would clear customs quicker.

Our Story

A vision to connect Jamaican produce with global markets.

ABM was founded in order to bridge the gap between premium Jamaican agricultural producers and international buyers. By analyzing market demands across borders, we recognized a major opportunity to establish an efficient, quality-driven export pipeline from our local farmer to the world.

1993

Rooted in Jamaica

Began operations as an independent distributor, successfully launching our first commercial export shipment of premium Jamaican mangoes.

2003

Wholesale Expansion

Scaled our logistics network to begin direct export to major international wholesale distributors.

2026

A Trusted Global Export Partner

Our operations have extensively expanded, securely exporting premium produce to a diverse portfolio of leading international wholesalers.

The export process

What happens after you book.

You share cargo details, dimensions and Incoterms. We confirm carrier space and issue a locked rate, usually within 24 hours.
Our brokers prepare the commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and HS classification ahead of loading — not after.
Cargo is palletised or containerised at our bonded facility, sealed, weighed and loaded onto the booked vessel, flight or truck.
You receive the bill of lading or airway bill plus a tracking link updated at every port, hub or border crossing.
Import clearance is filed ahead of arrival at destination, so cargo moves straight to pickup or last-mile delivery.

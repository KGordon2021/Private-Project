Export Services

Premium produce.
Sourced and exported fresh.

We specialize in exporting high-quality agricultural provisions directly from certified farms to global distributors. By managing the complete sourcing, grading, and preparation process, we utilize air transport to guarantee your cargo arrives with peak freshness and maximum shelf life.

End-to-End Air Logistics

From farm gates to flight dispatch.

1. Farm-Gate Sourcing

We procure fresh produce directly from our network of certified farmers just 1 to 2 days prior to your scheduled flight departure.

Direct Procurement · Ultra-Fresh

2. Specialized Produce Selection

We strictly grade and sort our produces—such as yams, sweet potatoes, breadfruit, and mangoes—to ensure perfect maturity and zero defects.

Yams · Sweet Potatoes · Breadfruit · Mangoes

3. Facility Processing

Arriving produce is swiftly moved to our hygienic facility for systematic washing, air-drying, and precision sorting into export batches.

Washing & Sorting · Clean Pack

4. Export-Grade Packing

Produce is packed inside customized, well-ventilated, cold-chain ready packaging designed to withstand altitude pressures and protect shelf life.

Ventilated Cartons · Air-Ready

5. Customs & Phyto Checks

We process all priority export paperwork, including essential agricultural inspections and phytosanitary certificates, before terminal arrival.

Phytosanitary · Export Customs

6. Air Freight Dispatch

Cargo is delivered to the airport terminal for immediate loading, moving seamlessly onto flights handled by you or booked by us.

Air Waybill (AWB) · Cargo Loading

The export process

What happens after confirming your produce.

Once your specific produce varieties and volumes are locked in, our export desk immediately coordinates the logistical schedule—whether aligning with your own airline booking or utilizing our preferred carrier space to secure seamless departure tracking.
We manage all critical agricultural compliance protocols. Our team schedules the mandatory inspections and secures your Phytosanitary Certificates and export documentation to ensure a seamless transition from the packing house to the tarmac.
To guarantee exceptional quality, we procure the agreed produce from our certified growers just 1 to 2 days before the flight. Items are immediately brought to our facility to be washed, graded, and packed in ventilated, export-ready cartons.
The prepared cargo is transferred securely to the airport freight terminal. Upon final handover to the airline, your Air Waybill (AWB) is issued, tracking your fresh produce as it transitions through the air.
Agricultural and customs clearances are finalized swiftly at the destination airport terminal, bypassing potential cargo delays so your pristine provisions can go straight to distributor cold storage or wholesale pickup.

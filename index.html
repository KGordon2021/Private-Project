How a shipment moves

From quote to takeoff, in four steps.

01

Quote & Confirm

Submit details to get an immediate quote within 2–4 hours. Once confirmed, we lock in flight space with your carrier or ours.

02

Harvest & Process

Produce is procured from farm gates 1–2 days before departure, then immediately washed, sorted, and packed into ventilated cartons.

03

Clear & Dispatch

Our team handles agricultural customs inspections, secures Phytosanitary Certificates, and delivers cargo to the airport terminal.

04

Fly & Handover

Your cargo is loaded onto the flight with an active Air Waybill (AWB) for tracking, ensuring priority arrival and cold-storage pickup.
